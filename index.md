I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute for Data Innovation in
Science, Seoul National University.

I study statistical methods for data in non-Euclidean spaces.
My research focuses on Fréchet means, statistical inference on manifolds,
and complex-valued factor models.

## Research interests

- Geometric statistics and Fréchet means
- Statistical inference on manifolds
- Complex-valued data and signal analysis
- Shape and compositional data analysis
