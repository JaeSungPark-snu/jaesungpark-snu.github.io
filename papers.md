## Work in progress

### Complex-Valued One-Factor Model and its Application to ENDOR Spectroscopy

**Jaesung Park**, Sungkyu Jung, and Yvo Pokern.  
*Manuscript in preparation.*

Estimation and inference for a common complex signal under phase and amplitude variation and anisotropic measurement noise, with applications to ENDOR spectroscopy.

### Wasserstein-Quantile PCA

**Jaesung Park** and Sungkyu Jung.  
*Manuscript in preparation.*

Dimension reduction for probability distributions using Wasserstein geometry and quantile functions.

### Diffusion means with jointly estimated diffusion time

*Ongoing project · Working title.*

Joint estimation of location and diffusion time on manifolds, with a focus on uniqueness, stability, and asymptotic inference.

### Data-adaptive Fréchet means and regression

*Ongoing project · Working title.*

Fréchet estimation and regression with cost functions learned from data, including adaptive robust losses and models for noisy non-Euclidean observations.
